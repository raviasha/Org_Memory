# Board Approval Memo — Q2 FY26 Capital Budget Authorization

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** CFO, CEO  
**Date:** February 2026  
**Re:** Capital Budget Authorization for FY26 Infrastructure Program  
**Classification:** Board Confidential

---

## Purpose

This memo requests Board approval for the FY26 Infrastructure Capital Budget envelope of up to **$20M** to be allocated across approved Q2 and Q3 infrastructure modernization projects.

## Background

Acme's current IT infrastructure is operating on hardware dating to 2019–2021. The IT leadership team has identified a critical modernization window in H2 FY26 to migrate workloads to cloud infrastructure and refresh aging on-premises equipment, reducing operational risk and enabling the next phase of product growth.

## Budget Request

| Category                     | Amount   |
|------------------------------|----------|
| Cloud migration (H2 FY26)    | $12.0M   |
| Hardware refresh              | $4.0M    |
| Professional services        | $2.5M    |
| Contingency reserve (10%)    | $1.5M    |
| **Total FY26 envelope**      | **$20.0M**|

## Authorization Requested

The Board is asked to approve:
1. The $20M capital budget envelope for FY26 infrastructure
2. Delegation of individual project approvals up to $5M to the CFO
3. Requirement that any single project above $5M return to the Board for final approval

## Governance

All projects drawing from this envelope must:
- Use the CFO-approved discount rate for NPV analysis
- Submit quarterly variance reports to the Finance Committee
- Obtain CFO sign-off before contract execution

## Resolution

**RESOLVED:** The Board approves the FY26 infrastructure capital budget envelope of $20M, with CFO delegation authority for individual projects up to $5M.
