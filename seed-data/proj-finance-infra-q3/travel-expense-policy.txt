1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes guidelines for business travel and expense reimbursement for all Acme Corp employees.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and consultants incurring business expenses on behalf of Acme Corp.

3. AIR TRAVEL
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours.
- Business class permitted for flights over 6 hours with VP approval.
- Book at least 14 days in advance when possible.
- Use the approved corporate travel portal (TripManager).

4. HOTEL
- Maximum $250/night in Tier 1 cities (San Francisco, New York, London).
- Maximum $180/night in Tier 2 cities.
- Use corporate rate when available.

5. MEALS AND ENTERTAINMENT
- Daily meal per diem: $75 (domestic), $100 (international).
- Team meals: up to $50 per person, manager approval required.
- Client entertainment: up to $150 per person, VP approval required.

6. GROUND TRANSPORTATION
- Use economy rideshare or public transit.
- Rental cars require manager pre-approval.
- Mileage reimbursement: current IRS rate.

7. EXPENSE REPORTING
- Submit all expenses within 30 days of travel.
- Original receipts required for all expenses over $25.
- Submit via Expensify with manager approval.

8. PERSONAL EXPENSES
Personal expenses are not reimbursable and must not be included in expense reports.
